Privacy Policy for Square Fall

1. Introduction

Welcome to Square Fall — an arcade-style reflex game for Android. This Privacy Policy describes how we ("Square Fall Studio", "we", "our", or "us") handle information when you download and play the game. By playing Square Fall, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

🎮

2. About Square Fall

Square Fall is a fast-paced, reflex-based arcade game where the player controls a falling square across a track. The game features:

  • Normal and Hardcore difficulty modes
  • Lives (hearts) system with combo multipliers
  • Fever Mode — a high-intensity bonus mode
  • Unlockable skins and cosmetic customizations
  • Daily missions and quest system
  • Achievements and progression tracking
  • High-score leaderboard (local)
  • In-game coin economy for unlocking content
  • Animated splash screen with studio branding
Square Fall is a fully offline game. No internet connection is required to play.
📋

3. Data We Collect

Square Fall is designed with privacy first. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers. All data generated by the game is stored exclusively on your device.

Data stored locally on your device includes:

  • High score and best score records
  • Total coins earned and spent
  • Selected difficulty mode preference
  • Unlocked skins and selected skin
  • Daily quest progress and completion status
  • Achievement unlock status and progress
  • Sound/music preference (muted or unmuted)
  • Total games played counter
None of the above data ever leaves your device. It is never uploaded, shared, or accessible to us or any third party.
🚫

4. Data We Do NOT Collect

We explicitly do not collect any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, or any account information
  • Phone number or device identifiers (IMEI, GAID, etc.)
  • Location data (GPS or network-based)
  • Camera, microphone, or media library access
  • Contacts or call logs
  • Browsing history or usage analytics
  • Financial or payment information
  • IP address or network traffic data
  • Crash logs or diagnostics sent to us
🔐

5. App Permissions

Square Fall requests only the minimum permissions necessary to function:

  • VIBRATE — for haptic feedback on game events (optional, can be disabled).
  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE — not required; all save data uses internal app storage.
Square Fall does not request INTERNET permission. The game is fully offline.
🔗

6. Third-Party Services

Square Fall (version 1.0) does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics services, advertising networks, or social media platforms. There are no ads in the game. There are no in-app purchases. We do not use:

  • Google AdMob / AdSense
  • Firebase Analytics / Crashlytics
  • Facebook SDK
  • Unity Ads / AppLovin / IronSource
  • Any cloud save or leaderboard services
👶

7. Children's Privacy

Square Fall is suitable for all ages, including children under 13. Because we do not collect any personal data whatsoever, the game is fully compliant with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and similar regulations worldwide.

Parents and guardians can allow their children to play Square Fall with full confidence that no personal information will be collected or shared.

🗑️

8. Deleting Your Data

All game data (scores, coins, achievements, settings) is stored locally in the app's private storage. You can delete all data at any time by:

  • Uninstalling Square Fall from your device
  • Going to Settings → Apps → Square Fall → Storage → Clear Data
Since no data is stored on our servers, uninstalling the app permanently and completely removes all associated data.
🛡️

9. Security

All game progress data is stored using Android's SharedPreferences in the app's sandboxed private storage, which is inaccessible to other apps on the device. Because we do not transmit any data over the internet, there is no risk of data interception or server-side breaches related to your gameplay.

⚖️

10. Your Rights

Under applicable privacy laws (including GDPR, CCPA, and similar regulations), you have the right to:

  • Know what data is collected about you (Section 3 covers this)
  • Delete your data (see Section 8)
  • Object to data processing — not applicable as we do not process personal data
  • Contact us with any privacy concerns at any time
